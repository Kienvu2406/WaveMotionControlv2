AUTO REV2 - UI layout correction

Cause of REV1 failure:
- Left/right scroll panels contained AutoSize TableLayoutPanel controls docked Top.
- Under real WinForms DPI/docking, preferred-size negotiation could collapse the AUTO page layout into a small region at top-left.

Fix:
- Root remains 3-column Dock=Fill: 310px | flexible grid | 310px.
- Center 16x16 grid always uses the flexible Fill column.
- Left/right sidebars remain independently scrollable.
- Sidebar content uses explicit heights and fixed TableLayoutPanel rows; AutoSize is NOT used.
- All primary buttons/inputs are Dock=Fill within rows sized for DPI scaling.
- AUTO REV1 motion logic is unchanged.
